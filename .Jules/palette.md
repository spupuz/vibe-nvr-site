## 2026-08-12 - Missing Focus Indicators in Vanilla HTML/CSS

**Learning:** Pure vanilla HTML/CSS projects often lack built-in focus states (like `:focus-visible`) that modern CSS frameworks typically provide out of the box, severely impacting keyboard accessibility.

**Action:** When working on vanilla projects without frameworks like Tailwind or Bootstrap, always explicitly check for and add global `:focus-visible` styles early in the styling process to ensure basic keyboard navigation is supported.
