Why VibeNVR?

Built for efficiency, privacy, and modern standards.

Storage Profiles

Support for multiple storage profiles with independent quotas, paths, and retention policies. SSD for speed, HDD for depth.

Device Discovery

Built-in ONVIF and WS-Discovery scanner automatically finds cameras on your network and extracts RTSP feeds.

Privacy First

Your data stays on your local network. No cloud subscriptions, no sensitive data tracking. You own your footage.

VibeEngine

Custom high-performance video engine built with OpenCV for efficient motion detection and recording.

Modern UI

A sleek, reactive interface built with React. Responsive design that works great on desktop and mobile.

Secure by Default

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA TOTP) with recovery codes. Authenticated media access. Designed to sit safely behind a reverse proxy.

Privacy Masking

Permanent black-out zones burned into recordings at the engine level to ensure absolute privacy and GDPR compliance.

Motion Zones

Define custom exclusion zones to ignore areas with constant motion, drastically reducing false alerts from trees or traffic.

Hardware Accel

Supports NVIDIA, Intel QSV, and AMD VAAPI for efficient video processing on compatible hardware.

Backup & Restore

Automated and manual configuration backups. Safely export and restore all cameras, settings, and user profiles.

Containerized

Runs everywhere Docker runs. Modular architecture ensuring stability and easy updates.
