## 2026-09-04 - Paired Interactive States
**Learning:** In CSS, interactive pseudo-classes like `:hover` and `:focus-visible` should always be paired with an `:active` state. Without an `:active` state, users receive no visual tactile feedback that their click or tap action is actually registering, which degrades the perceived responsiveness of the interface. This codebase often had `:hover` and `:focus-visible` defined for buttons and links, but missed the `:active` counterpart for general anchor tags, mobile toggles, lightbox close buttons, and footer links.
**Action:** Always review interactive elements for the full suite of pseudo-classes (`:hover`, `:focus-visible`, and `:active`). Apply subtle visual changes like `transform: scale()` or `opacity` changes on `:active` to simulate physical interaction.
